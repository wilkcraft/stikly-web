Privacy Policy

🔒 No data collected
Effective date: October 27, 2026

At Stikly, we take your privacy very seriously. This policy describes how we handle your information.

1Information We Collect

Stikly does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information. All notes and data you create are stored locally and exclusively on your own device (PC). We do not have access to them.

Your notes are saved as individual .json files in a notes folder inside the application's data folder (%APPDATA%\stikly\notes), together with a small settings file that remembers your theme preference. When you delete a note from the notes panel, the file is moved to your Recycle Bin. You can back up, move or delete these files at any time. Uninstalling Stikly does not remove them.

2Use of Information

Since we do not collect any data, we do not use your information for any purpose. All information you generate remains on your device and under your exclusive control.

3Third-Party Services

Our application does not use third-party services (such as analytics, advertising, or tracking) that could collect information about you.

This applies to the Stikly application. Our website loads its fonts from Google Fonts, which may receive your IP address when you visit the site. The website also stores your theme preference (Auto, Light or Dark) in your browser's local storage; this value never leaves your device.

4Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new policy on this page. We recommend that you review it periodically.
